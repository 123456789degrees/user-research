# UA1 — Opportunity Notes

## Friction observed

Tennis players who track their matches and opponents currently do so in physical journals. The physical format:
- Cannot surface patterns across matches
- Cannot give pre-match tips based on prior history with a specific opponent
- Requires manual lookup and interpretation before rematches

## Conversations

**2026-06-04 — Student (project owner)**

The student plays recreational/competitive tennis and keeps a physical tennis journal. After matches, they manually log:
- Match score
- Opponent's strengths and weaknesses
- Their own performance (what went well, what didn't)

The key unmet need: when facing the same opponent again, the student wants to recall and act on everything they learned last time — but has to manually flip through pages and synthesize it themselves. They want an AI that can read that history and give them targeted tips before the rematch.

## Candidate opportunity

**Who:** Tennis players (recreational to competitive) who already have the habit of reflecting on matches.

**Problem:** Match notes live in a static medium (paper journal) that can't actively help the player prepare for rematches or spot patterns over time.

**Current workaround:** Physical tennis journal — proves the behavior exists and there's motivation, but the format limits what you can do with the data.

**Differentiating idea:** AI-powered opponent scouting — the app reads your logged history against a specific opponent and generates pre-match preparation tips.

## Open questions

- Do other tennis players beyond the student keep match journals? How many?
- What format works best for logging mid/post-match notes quickly (structured fields vs. free text)?
- Would players want social features (comparing notes with teammates) or is this purely personal?
- What existing apps (UTR, GameSetMatch, etc.) are players already using, and what gap do they leave?
